## Sound
Original score, synthesised in numpy by `trailer/scripts/score.py` (pad, pulse bass, piano theme, formant choir,
taiko and snare, brass stabs, bells, booms, risers, a ticking clock), arranged bar by bar to the cut. No samples,
no third-party audio. Mastering: a 20 ms fade-in on the first impact, a glue limiter, measured gain to -14 LUFS and
a 4x-oversampled limiter, then AAC. The captures are silent (canvas capture), so there are no in-game SFX.

## Open, needs a human
- **Watch it with sound.** The mix was checked by measurement only (loudness per bar follows the plan: quiet
  memories, building escalation, the climax ~5 dB over it), not by ear.
- **"Directly screen-recorded":** the gameplay is the game's own deterministic canvas capture (WebCodecs), not an
  OS screen recording, so the HUD (health bars, damage numbers) is not in frame. Runs are deterministic, so screen
  recording the same seeds reproduces the footage frame for frame if the organisers want a literal screen capture.
- **Delivery:** upload to Google Drive with "Anyone with the link: Viewer" only after the team approves.
