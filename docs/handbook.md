## Technical requirements

**Tools:** any game engine, along with any combination of coding agents, tools, CLIs and MCPs.

**Plagiarism:** blatant plagiarism results in disqualification. This includes direct copying of assets or source
code, and copying entire mechanics without any uniqueness.

## Submission
1. Google Drive link to the trailer video
2. Public GitHub repo link with all source code and assets
3. Deployed game URL (optional)

## The trailer
- Evaluation is based on a **three-minute trailer**.
- **Two of the three minutes must be actual gameplay, directly screen-recorded.**
- Stylistic editing and graphics (teaser-trailer style) are encouraged for one minute, and can be spread
  throughout the trailer.
- The trailer must detail **exactly how the themes were incorporated** into the game.
- The initial evaluation is done **solely on the trailer**.

## Evaluation
Three themes are announced on the day of the event. At least one must be used; using all three is not required.

**Theme adherence: 30%**
- Core integration: 15
- Multi-theme synergy: 10
- Atmospheric framing: 5

**The rest of the evaluation:**
- Core gameplay and feel
- Visual polish
- Audio design
- Innovation and uniqueness
- Technical stability
- Scope and completeness
- Trailer quality

The top 5 games chosen from the trailers are shortlisted for the judges to play and select the winners.
