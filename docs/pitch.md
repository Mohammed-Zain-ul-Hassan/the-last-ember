THE LAST EMBER: the pitch (about 1:45 read aloud)

What if one tiny flame was the only thing stopping time?

A whole city froze one second before a flood. Everyone stopped, mid-step, mid-breath. You carry the last flame in the world, and you are walking through that frozen city.

Act One, the Still City. Your flame makes shadows, just like a real torch. Hold it at the right angle to a frozen person, and their shadow wakes up. It replays the last ten seconds of their life. Watch what they did, step into their memory, and do it yourself: pull a lever, open a door, cross a canal on your own shadow. But one shadow is wrong. It only moves when you look away.

Act Two, the Hearth. You set the flame down in an old fireplace, and for the first time in four thousand years the sun sets. Now monsters crawl out of the shadows the fire throws. You fight them hour by hour, from 7 PM until dawn.

Fire, Shadow and Time are one idea. Fire makes the shadows. Shadows hold the past. And moving the flame decides what happens next.

The twist? That wrong shadow knows you. Better than you know yourself. And at dawn, you choose what the light is worth.

Try it like this:
1. Start Act One. Hold the flame up to the first frozen guard until his shadow wakes, then hold E to step into his memory.
2. Open the Chapters and jump to 9 PM to fight at the hearth.
3. Then 3 AM, for the Hollow Marshal.

Controls: WASD to move. Move the mouse to look and aim the flame. E to wake a shadow. Shift to draw the flame back. Click to strike. Esc to pause.

One flame. One night. Keep it alive.
