# Concepts: Shadow / Fire / Time

Brief: all three themes allowed, team going all-in on creativity. Kickoff 10:50 Day 1.

## Theme readings (literal / metaphorical)
- **Shadow**: cast shadows, silhouettes, darkness, stealth, shadow puppets / past self, a copy that follows you, hidden side, dread, "in the shadow of".
- **Fire**: flame, the sun, burning, ignition, light source / fuel, passion, destruction-renewal, urgency, consumption.
- **Time**: clock, time of day, rewind, decay, candle clocks / memory, delay, rhythm, patience, a budget you spend.

Key overlaps: a **sundial** (the sun's fire casts the shadow that tells time) and a **candle clock** (fire burning
down measures time and casts shadows). Each one is all three themes at once.

## Concepts
1. **Sundial**: you are a living shadow and sunlight burns you. Hold a key to scrub the time of day: the sun swings
   across the sky and every shadow in town rotates and stretches with it. Build shadow-bridges and cross to the exit.
   One input (time) moves the fire (sun), which moves the shadows (your ground). *All 3 in one mechanic.*
2. **Last Light**: you are a candle spirit in a ruin at night. Your light casts real-time shadows from the pillars,
   and shadow creatures can only travel through the darkness you create. Your wax is the clock until dawn. Flare to
   burn them, but it burns minutes. *All 3.*
3. **Afterburn**: your shadow follows your exact path 2 s late, and anything it touches burns. Lure enemies across
   your own past. *All 3 (delay = time, shadow = weapon, fire = damage).*
4. **Clockwork Shade**: a candle inside a clock tower; the rotating hands cast shadows on the wall that you walk on as
   platforms. *All 3, but polygon platforming physics.*
5. **Unburn**: fire spreads through a village; your hourglass rewinds time in a radius to un-burn houses. *Fire+Time,
   shadow is only a skin.*
6. **Ashen Echo**: 10 s time loops; past runs replay as shadows that hold switches. *Dropped: near-clone of
   The Company of Myself / time-clone puzzlers.*

## Hard filters
- 6 dropped (near-clone).
- 5: shadow is skin only, so it stays for comparison but can't win.
- 4: polygon-collision platforming on moving shadows is a physics risk; Buildable is scored low.

Closest existing games: Sundial ~ *Schim* (hop between shadows) but with no time control; the twist of scrubbing the
sun is ours. Last Light ~ survivors-likes + *Alan Wake* light-burns-dark; the twist is enemies routing through the
shadows you cast. Afterburn ~ snake / echo trails.

**Recommendation: 1, Sundial.** It is the most original idea and the only one where a single button press shows
all three themes at once, which is exactly what the synergy points (10) and a creativity-first judging panel reward.
Its weak spot is moment-to-moment fun, which is mitigated in the spec (the sun moves on its own, short levels,
burn-grace sprints).
**Runner-up: 2, Last Light.** It's the better arcade loop but less original.
